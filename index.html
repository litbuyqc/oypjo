What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list that can organize shopping links, categories, prices, sizes and notes. Use it to compare options, then check the original listing before buying. A spreadsheet entry alone does not verify quality, availability or authenticity.

02 / A useful shortlist

How to use an
Oopbuy spreadsheet

A useful Oopbuy shopping guide starts with comparable details. Record the same information for each candidate so your decision rests on more than a thumbnail.

Find your starting point ↗

  1. Choose one category first

    Begin with shoes, hoodies, T-shirts or accessories. Keeping similar items together makes differences in fit, construction and cost easier to spot.

  2. Record the original listing

    Save the product URL, seller name, exact color, selected size and the date you checked the listing. Separate a seller’s stated details from your own observations.

  3. Compare measurements and evidence

    Read the size chart, review material information and inspect any available product or quality-check photos. Ask for missing measurements before ordering.

  4. Calculate the complete cost

    Consider the item price, delivery charges, service fees, currency conversion and applicable taxes. Confirm current checkout details and return conditions.

05 / Clear answers

Before you
open another tab.

Practical answers to common Oopbuy spreadsheet questions, from sizing to the meaning of a listed price.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list that can organize product links, categories, prices, sizes and shopping notes. Its accuracy depends on the person maintaining it; it is not proof of product quality or authenticity.

How do I use an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

Choose a category, open the original listing, record the exact variant and seller, compare measurements, and estimate your full purchase cost before ordering.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check whether the listed amount includes domestic delivery, international shipping, service fees, currency conversion and applicable taxes.

How can I check clothing and shoe sizing?

Compare the listing’s measurements with an item you already own. For clothing, check chest, length, shoulder and waist measurements. For shoes, check the seller’s size chart and foot-length guidance.

Are branded products in a spreadsheet automatically authentic?

No. A brand name, photograph or spreadsheet entry does not verify authenticity. Check seller credentials, product provenance, return terms and any available independent authentication.

Is this the official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS category pages and the CAFANS homepage.
